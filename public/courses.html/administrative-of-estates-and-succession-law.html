TOPIC ONE: INTRODUCTION

Meanin of Succession refer to the passing of whole or substantial part of the estate of one person to another person or persons. Also, it can be define as the transimission of the estate of one person to another person(s) after death through the operation of laws.
Succession law - Is the branch of private law which regulate the transfer of estate from one person to another person's, succession laws was divided in several categories which are:-
1. Customary law
2. Common law
3. Legislation (Statutory)
4. Islamic law
5. Hindu law

Administration of estate - refer to the whole process where by person representative of the deceased (administrator/excutor) get the powers by the approval of the estate of the deceased after the occurance of death.“Administrator” define under section 2 of Probate and Administration of Estate Act, Cap 352, R.E 2023, means a person appointed by the court to administer the estate of a deceased person when there is no executor or no executor is able and willing to act, and includes, when Part VIII applies and subject to the provisions thereof, a person appointed an administrator under that Part. Administration of estate may occur tested/intested.

Tested Succession - This refer to the situation where a person died living behind a valid will such a will must account for property or the estate also, it must account on how the estate must be divided to the heirs, but also, such will must name a person representative who will take charge of the administration of estate. A person mention in the will to administer the estate of deceased is known as an excutor (a male administrator) or an excutrix (a female administrator), “executor” defined under section 2 of Probate and Administration of Estate Act, Cap 352, R.E 2023 to means a person to whom the execution of the last will of a deceased person is, by the testator’s appointment, confided;

Intested succession - refer to the situation where a deceased person has not left a valid will that means intested succession occur whenever a deceased person has not accounted for the list of his propert (estate), the names and the number of the heirs and a person to administer the estate. Intested succession, it is a duty of the court to appoint person representation of the deceased it means an administrator (a male person representative) or an admistatrix (a female person representative).
Generally; where there is a tesed succession there be grant of probate to the excutor or executrix while there is intested succession the will be grant of letter of administration to an administrator or an administatrix. For probate and letter of administration to e granted there must be aprove of death, it is important that the death of a person claim to have passed away to be registered and legal acknowledge.

The prove of death in Tanzania can be happen in two forms
(i) Through death certificate issued by (RITA)
As the requirement of the birth and deaths registration Act
(ii) Through judicial order of declaration of death
This occur where there is a presumption of death or where there is presumed deceased as per the requirement of the law of marriage Act.

TOPIC THREE: WILL

Section 2 of Probate and Administration of Estate Act, Cap 352, R.E 2023, define “Will” means the legal declaration of the intentions of a testator with respect to his property, which he desires to be carried into effect after his death. A will must explain the disposition of a property that a person owned at a time of death, will sould contain disposition/ distribution of his asset after his death. A will can be either written or oral, section 25 of Probate and Administration of Estate Act, Cap 352, R.E 2023, recognized both written and oral will.
Islamic law also, define will as is the word originated as Wasiyyah, it means a direction by which a person directhis heirs or personal representative regardin the distribution of his property after death. A will can be either single document or a class of several document, a will and it's suprement or a will and it's attachment. A suprement to a will is hnown as Codicil which define to means an instrument made in relation to a will, and explaining, altering or adding to its dispositions; section 2 of Probate and Administration of Estate Act, Cap 352, R.E 2023, or in other word may be define as instrument or document in relation to a will with the purpose of explaining ultering or adding to it's disposition.
A will and codicil both have legal effect before the eyes of law, for the reason that the codicil is of the similar nature to a will due to the purpose being served within a will or codicil, but also, due to the formality that exist within a codicil and will. Therefore, a will can be a single document or several document.
Purpose of Codicil
i. To make addition
ii. Variations
iii. Revocations/ Modification to a will previously made by the same person
These two may distinguished as Will is the main document while Codicil is addition/ subwill to adding beneficiary, made modification or variation of the main will. Codicil, must refer to the date and mention the first will form a single document. It is not necessary for witness or beneficiary to be the same in the will and codicil but should be written by the same estate owner.
