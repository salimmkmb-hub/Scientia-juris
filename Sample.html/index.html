LAND LAW Mfumo wa Sheria za Tanzania

Land Tenure Systems & Property Rights

Topic 1: Land Tenure System in Tanzania

In Tanzania, all land is public land vested in the President as trustee for and on behalf of all citizens. Land tenure is broadly categorized into Granted Rights of Occupancy and Customary Rights of Occupancy.

📌 Land Act, Cap. 113 [R.E. 2019] - Section 4
"All land in Tanzania is public land vested in the President as trustee for and on behalf of the citizens of Tanzania."

Topic 2: Customary vs Granted Rights

A Granted Right of Occupancy is issued by the Commissioner for Lands for a term up to 99 years, whereas a Customary Right of Occupancy is granted by Village Councils under Cap. 114.
