LABOUR LAW Sheria za Kazi na Mahusiano Kazini - Tanzania Mainland

Employment Standards & Labour Relations Framework

Topic 1: Fundamental Principles & Rights at Work

Labour Law in Tanzania sets out the minimum statutory rights and protections for employees. The legal framework strictly prohibits forced labour, child labour, discrimination in employment, and interference with the freedom of association.

📌 Employment and Labour Relations Act, Cap. 366 [R.E. 2019] - Section 7(1)
"Every employer shall ensure that he promotes equal opportunity in employment and strives to eliminate discrimination in any employment policy or practice."

Discrimination is prohibited on grounds including race, gender, nationality, political opinion, religion, disability, HIV/AIDS status, or marital status.

Topic 2: Contracts of Employment & Minimum Standards

A contract of employment can be formed orally or in writing. However, for contracts of specified duration or specific tasks, statutory conditions apply.

1. Types of Employment Contracts (Section 14)

  • Contract for an unspecified period of time (Permanent contract).
  • Contract for a specified period for professionals or managerial cadre.
  • Contract for a specific task.

2. Basic Basic Working Conditions (Part III)

Sheria inainainisha viwango vya chini vya mazingira ya kazi:

  • Hours of Work (Section 19): Maximum 45 ordinary hours in any week (9 hours per day for a 5-day week or 8 hours per day for a 6-day week).
  • Annual Leave (Section 31): Minimum of 28 consecutive days on full remuneration in any leave cycle.
  • Maternity & Paternity Leave (Sections 33 & 34): 84 days paid maternity leave (or 100 days for multiple births) and 3 days paid paternity leave.

Topic 3: Termination of Employment & Unfair Dismissal

For a termination of employment to be lawful under Tanzanian law, it must meet two essential conditions: **Substantive Fairness** (valid reason) and **Procedural Fairness** (fair procedure).

📌 Employment and Labour Relations Act, Cap. 366 - Section 37(1)
"It shall be unlawful for an employer to terminate the employment of an employee unfairly."

Valid Reasons for Termination (Section 37(2)):

  • Relating to the employee's conduct (Misconduct).
  • Relating to the employee's capacity or performance (Incapacity).
  • Based on operational requirements of the employer (Retrenchment/Redundancy).
⚖️ Precedent Case: Air Tanzania Corporation v. Peter Mawalla (2001) TLR 110
Mahakama ilisisitiza kuwa hata kama mwajiri ana sababu ya msingi ya kumfuta kazi mfanyakazi, kukosa kufuata taratibu za kisheria za usikilizaji wa haki (Right to be heard / Natural Justice) kunaifanya utenguzi huo kuwa batili na usio wa haki (Unfair Dismissal).

Topic 4: Dispute Resolution Mechanisms

Disputes arising in employment are resolved through statutory bodies established under the Labour Institutions Act, Cap. 300.

The primary forum for mediation and arbitration is the Commission for Mediation and Arbitration (CMA). If a party is dissatisfied with an arbitration award, an application for revision can be lodged in the Labour Division of the High Court of Tanzania.
