INVESTMENT LAW Sheria ya Uwekezaji - Tanzania

Legal Framework for Investment & Incentives

Topic 1: Legal & Institutional Framework (TIC)

Investment Law in Tanzania provides the primary legal regime governing domestic and foreign investments. The principal legislation is the Tanzania Investment Act, 2022, which established the Tanzania Investment Centre (TIC) as the primary agency of the Government to coordinate, encourage, promote, and facilitate investment.

📌 Tanzania Investment Act, 2022 - Section 4
"The Centre shall continue to exist as the primary agency of the Government to coordinate, encourage, promote and facilitate investment in Tanzania Mainland and to advise the Government on investment policy and related matters."

Scope of Application: The Act applies to local and foreign investments that meet the specified minimum capital investment thresholds to obtain a Certificate of Incentive.

Topic 2: Investment Guarantees & Protection

The legal framework guarantees protection to investors against nationalization or compulsory acquisition of their properties.

1. Guarantee Against Compulsory Acquisition

No enterprise or interest in an enterprise shall be compulsorily acquired by the Government except for public interest, and in accordance with the law providing for prompt, fair, and adequate compensation.

📌 Tanzania Investment Act, 2022 - Section 27
"Subject to Article 24 of the Constitution, no business enterprise or an interest in a business enterprise shall be compulsory acquired or nationalized by the Government..."

2. Unconditional Transferability of Funds

Foreign investors are guaranteed unconditional transferability through any authorized dealer bank in freely convertible currency of net profits, dividends, debt service payments, and proceeds from liquidation of the enterprise.

Topic 3: Investment Incentives & Certificate of Incentive

Investors who register their projects with the Tanzania Investment Centre (TIC) and meet the minimum investment thresholds are granted a Certificate of Incentive, unlocking strategic economic benefits.

  • Minimum Capital Threshold (Local Investor): Minimum USD 50,000 (or equivalent in Tanzanian Shillings).
  • Minimum Capital Threshold (Foreign Investor): Minimum USD 500,000.

Benefits & Incentives include: Import duty exemptions on capital goods, concessional tax rates, automatic immigration quotas for key expatriate staff, and access to One-Stop Facilitation Centre services.

⚖️ Precedent / International Arbitration Case: Biwater Gauff (Tanzania) Ltd v. United Republic of Tanzania (ICSID Case No. ARB/05/22)
Shauri maarufu la kimataifa (ICSID Arbitration) linalofundisha msingi wa haki za wawekezaji wa kigeni (Expropriation & Fair and Equitable Treatment - FET) dhidi ya hatua za serikali katika miradi ya miundombinu.

Topic 4: Dispute Settlement Mechanisms

Where a dispute arises between a foreign investor and the Government or TIC, the law promotes amicable resolution through negotiation.

If negotiation fails, the dispute may be submitted to arbitration in accordance with:

  • Arbitration laws of Tanzania.
  • International Centre for Settlement of Investment Disputes (ICSID) framework.
  • Within the framework of any bilateral or multilateral agreement on investment protection agreed upon by Tanzania and the investor's country.
