CAPITAL MARKETS & SECURITIES LAW Masoko ya Mitaji na Dhamana - Legal & Regulatory Framework

Capital Markets Regulation, Securities & Corporate Finance

Topic 1: Legal & Institutional Framework (CMSA & DSE)

Capital Markets Law regulates the issuing, trading, and dealing in financial securities such as shares, bonds, and debentures. In Tanzania, the primary legislation is the Capital Markets and Securities Act, Cap. 79.

Key Regulatory Institutions:

  • CMSA (Capital Markets and Securities Authority): The statutory body established under Section 6 of Cap. 79 to promote, develop, and regulate the securities industry, protecting investor interests and ensuring market integrity.
  • DSE (Dar es Salaam Stock Exchange): The licensed stock exchange operating as a secondary market platform where listed securities (shares and bonds) are traded among investors.
📌 Capital Markets and Securities Act, Cap. 79 [R.E. 2019] - Section 10
"The functions of the Authority shall be to inquire into the affairs of any person dealing in securities, license and supervise stock exchanges, register prospectuses, and protect the interest of investors in securities."

Topic 2: Public Offers, Prospectus & IPOs

When a public company seeks to raise capital from the public by issuing shares or debentures (Initial Public Offer - IPO), it must strictly comply with statutory disclosure rules to prevent fraud and misrepresentation.

1. Requirement of a Prospectus

No person is permitted to issue an invitation to the public to subscribe for securities unless the invitation is accompanied by a Prospectus registered and approved by the CMSA.

📌 Capital Markets and Securities Act, Cap. 79 - Section 122
A prospectus must contain all material information that investors and their professional advisers would reasonably require to make an informed assessment of the assets, financial position, profits, losses, and prospects of the issuing company.

2. Civil and Criminal Liability for Misstatements

Directors, promoters, and experts who authorize the issue of a prospectus containing false, misleading, or deceptive statements are subject to both civil liability (paying compensation for losses) and criminal penalties under Cap. 79.

⚖️ Precedent Case: Derry v. Peek (1889) 14 App Cas 337
Kesi ya msingi inayofundisha udanganyifu (Fraudulent Misrepresentation) kwenye taarifa za kibiashara. Mahakama ilifafanua kuwa taarifa ya uongo iliyotolewa kwa kujua, bila kuamini ukweli wake, au kwa kutojali iwapo ni ya kweli au ya uongo, inajenga kosa la udanganyifu.

Topic 3: Market Intermediaries & Licensing

To ensure fair and transparent operations, no person can carry on business as a securities dealer, investment adviser, or fund manager without obtaining a license from the CMSA.

Key Licensed Intermediaries:

  • Licensed Dealers / Stockbrokers: Licensed members of the DSE authorized to execute buy and sell orders on behalf of investors.
  • Investment Advisers & Fund Managers: Entities licensed to manage investment portfolios or provide investment advice.
  • Central Depository (CSDR): The electronic registry system that holds securities in dematerialized (digital) form and settles transactions.

Topic 4: Market Offences & Insider Trading

Part XII of the Capital Markets and Securities Act, Cap. 79 creates strict statutory offences to prohibit market manipulation and preserve public confidence in financial markets.

1. Insider Trading (Matumizi ya Taarifa za Ndani) - Section 138

Occurs when an "insider" (e.g., director, executive, auditor, or connected person) who possesses price-sensitive, non-public information uses that information to buy or sell securities, or tips off others to trade.

📌 Capital Markets and Securities Act, Cap. 79 - Section 138(1)
"A person who is, or at any time in the preceding six months has been, connected with a body corporate shall not deal in any securities of that body corporate if he is in possession of information that is not generally available to the public..."

2. Market Manipulation & False Trading

  • False Trading & Market Rigging (Section 132): Creating a false or misleading appearance of active trading or artificial stock prices.
  • Fraudulently Inducing Persons to Deal in Securities (Section 135): Making false, misleading, or deceptive statements or forecasts to induce another person to buy or sell securities.
⚖️ Regulatory Landmark: SEC v. Texas Gulf Sulphur Co. (401 F.2d 833)
Kesi mashuhuri ya kimataifa inayoweka msingi wa kuzuia Insider Trading, ambapo mahakama iliamuru kuwa mtu yeyote mwenye taarifa za ndani za kampuni ambazo bado hazijatolewa kwa umma lazima azitangaze kwanza kwa umma au ajiepushe kabisa na kufanya biashara ya hisa hizo (Disclose or Abstain Rule).
