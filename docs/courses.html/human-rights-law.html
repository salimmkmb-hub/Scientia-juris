Topic 2: International Development Stages

Development of human rights law at the international level is divided into three major stages:

  • First Stage (1900–1972): Focus was mainly on conservation and natural resources.
  • Second Stage (1972–1992): Emergence of human rights law (Universal Declaration of Human Rights, ICCPR).
  • Third Stage (1992–Present): Strengthening of human rights mechanisms and regional frameworks.
