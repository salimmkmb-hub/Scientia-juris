INTERNATIONAL TRADE & FINANCE LAW Biashara na Fedha za Kimataifa - Legal Framework

International Trade, Financing Mechanisms & Dispute Settlement

Topic 1: Multilateral Trading System & WTO Principles

International Trade Law regulates cross-border movement of goods, services, and capital. The global trading system is governed by the World Trade Organization (WTO) under key legal agreements including the GATT (General Agreement on Tariffs and Trade) and GATS (General Agreement on Trade in Services).

Core Legal Principles of the WTO Framework:

  • Most-Favoured-Nation (MFN) Treatment (GATT Article I): Requires WTO members to accord any privilege, favor, or immunity granted to one country to all other WTO member states immediately and unconditionally.
  • National Treatment Principle (GATT Article III): Prohibits member states from discriminating between imported goods and domestically produced like goods once they have entered the domestic market (in terms of internal taxes or regulations).
  • Prohibition of Quantitative Restrictions (GATT Article XI): Prohibits quotas, import bans, or licenses, favoring tariffs as the transparent means of trade protection.
📌 GATT 1994 - Article III:4 (National Treatment)
"The products of the territory of any contracting party imported into the territory of any other contracting party shall be accorded treatment no less favourable than that accorded to like products of national origin..."

Topic 2: International Sales Contracts & Incoterms®

Contracts for the international sale of goods are governed internationally by the UN Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods (CISG 1980) and standard trade terms published by the International Chamber of Commerce (ICC) known as Incoterms®.

1. CISG Core Rules

  • Formation of Contract: Contract is concluded when an offer meets an unqualified acceptance (Rules on offer and acceptance).
  • Breach & Remedies: Distinguishes between fundamental breach (allowing contract termination) and non-fundamental breach (allowing damages or price reduction).

2. Essential Incoterms® Rules

Incoterms define the division of costs, risks, and responsibilities between buyer and seller:

  • FOB (Free On Board): Seller delivers goods on board the vessel nominated by the buyer. Risk passes when goods are on board.
  • CIF (Cost, Insurance, and Freight): Seller pays cost, freight, and marine insurance to the destination port, but risk transfers once goods are loaded on the vessel.
  • EXW (Ex Works): Minimum obligation for seller; buyer bears all costs and risks from the seller's premises.

Topic 3: International Trade Finance & Letters of Credit

Financing international sales involves mitigating credit and payment risks across jurisdictions. The primary payment mechanism is the Documentary Letter of Credit (L/C), governed globally by the ICC rules known as UCP 600 (Uniform Customs and Practice for Documentary Credits).

📌 Fundamental Principles of Letters of Credit (UCP 600):
1. Principle of Autonomy: The L/C is a separate and independent transaction from the underlying sales contract between buyer and seller.
2. Doctrine of Strict Compliance: Banks deal with documents, not with goods. Banks will pay only if the presented documents comply strictly on their face with the L/C terms.
⚖️ Precedent Case: United City Merchants v. Royal Bank of Canada [1983] 1 AC 168
Mahakama ya Baraza la Mabwana (House of Lords) ilithibitisha Principle of Autonomy na kueleza kuwa benki lazima ilipe chini ya Letter of Credit ikiwa nyaraka zipo sahihi, isipokuwa tu iwapo kuna ushahidi wa wazi wa udanganyifu (Fraud Exception) unaofanywa na muuzaji.

Topic 4: Dispute Settlement in International Trade

Disputes in international trade and finance are resolved through institutionalized international arbitration or specialized dispute bodies.

1. WTO Dispute Settlement Mechanism

Monitors legal disputes between sovereign states regarding compliance with WTO agreements through the Dispute Settlement Body (DSB), utilizing Panel Reports and Appellate Body rulings.

2. International Commercial Arbitration

Private disputes between cross-border commercial parties are commonly submitted to arbitration (e.g., ICC Court of Arbitration, LCIA). Enforcement of foreign arbitral awards is guaranteed globally under the 1958 New York Convention on the Recognition and Enforcement of Foreign Arbitral Awards.

⚖️ Statutory Enforcement in Tanzania: Arbitration Act, Cap. 15 [R.E. 2020]
Sheria ya Maamuzi ya Suluhisho (Arbitration) ya Tanzania inatambua na kuwezesha utekelezaji wa tuzo za suluhisho za kimataifa (Foreign Arbitral Awards) zilizotolewa chini ya misingi ya New York Convention.
