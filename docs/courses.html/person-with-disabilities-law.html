INTRODUCTION AND HISTORICAL BACKGROUND OF PERSON WITH DISSABILITIES

Section 63(1) of Person with Dissabilities Act, 2010 Section 2 of Dissabled Person (Employment) Act, define Disabled Person as "A person who on account of injury, disease or congenital deformity, is substantially handicapped obtaining employment, or in undertaking work on his own account, of kind which apart from that injury, disease or deformity would be suited to his age, experience and qualification" [According to this law at that time allow institution not to employ dissabled person that was according to law].
Aims of making that law in 1982
i. To protect disabled person un employment.
ii. To allow them to employed under institution.
iii. To introduce disabled with their rights.
iv. To ensure non-discrimination in the employment sector.
