Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Welcome to Scientia Juris. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or use our legal educational platform.

We may collect information about you in a variety of ways, including:

  • Personal Identification Data: Name, email address, phone number, or academic credentials when you register for an account, subscribe to newsletters, or contact us.

  • Cookies and Tracking Technologies: Small data files stored on your device to enhance site navigation, analyze performance, and improve your user experience.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for specific, lawful purposes, including:

  • Providing, operating, and maintaining our legal resources and course notes.
  • Managing your account and personal preferences.
  • Sending administrative updates, newsletter alerts, or responses to your legal queries.
  • Monitoring and analyzing usage trends to optimize web layout and performance.
  • Preventing fraudulent activities, unauthorized access, and cyber threats.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information only under the following limited circumstances:

  • Third-Party Service Providers: Trusted platforms that assist in web hosting, analytics, database management, or email delivery (under strict confidentiality agreements).
  • Legal Requirements: If required to do so by law, court order, or governmental regulations in accordance with the laws of the United Republic of Tanzania.

5. Data Security

We implement technical, administrative, and organizational security measures (such as SSL encryption and secure database controls) to guard your personal data against loss, misuse, unauthorized access, or disclosure.

6. Your Data Protection Rights

Depending on applicable data protection laws, you have the following rights:

  • Access: The right to request copies of your personal data held by us.
  • Correction: The right to request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Deletion: The right to request erasure of your personal data under certain conditions.
  • Opt-Out: The right to unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time via the link provided in our emails.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect operational, legal, or regulatory changes. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Effective Date".
